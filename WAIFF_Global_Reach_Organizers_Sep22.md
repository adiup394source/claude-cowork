# WAiFF India: organiser candidates with proven INTERNATIONAL operations

Research date: 22 Sep 2026. Method: WebSearch plus WebFetch on the URLs cited in brackets. No LinkedIn access, no sign-ups, nothing sent to anyone.

**Brief:** Aditya said the 19 Sep round of 34 Mumbai event/IP companies "were not big enough." This list only includes candidates that clear a second bar on top of Mumbai scale: **a verifiable track record of running or licensing events/IP outside India**, not just running big events inside India. Every claim below has a cited source; where a claim could not be verified (e.g. a brand's "official license" status), that is flagged rather than assumed.

**Excluded:** everything in the do-not-contact list (Wizcraft, Exhicon, Exhibitions India, IA-Meetings, Mach, MCI, Inc42, IIFA, FICCI, JioStar, InVideo, Jio World Centre, ByteDance/CapCut, Eros International, ZEE5, Whistling Woods International, government/government-funded bodies) and everything already in the 34-company list from `/tmp/waiff/WAIFF_Apollo_Stack_and_Mumbai_Organizers.md` (IFP, BookMyShow Live, NODWIN Gaming, Sunburn, Percept, Kyoorius, Adgully, Social Samosa, Pocket Aces, Skyesports, S8UL, exchange4media, Aegis/Bharat AI Innovation, TEAM/Mumbai Tech Week, Collective Artists Network/Galleri5, LocalHost/MAFF, Cineyug, Qyuki, Rooter, E-Factor Experiences, Fountainhead MKTG, DNA Entertainment Networks, RPSG Lifestyle Media, Network18, Hungama Digital Media, OML Entertainment, Kommune, Goafest/AAAI, JetSynthesys, Upthrust Esports, Kuku, Indiantelevision.com, EVA Live, Laqshya Media Group).

**Flags to carry forward:**
- **ZEE5** is excluded by name; **Zee Entertainment Enterprises Ltd (ZEEL)**, the listed parent that owns Zee Cine Awards and the Zee TV channel network, is a distinct corporate entity and is not on the exclusion list. Included below, flagged for your judgement call.
- **JioStar** and **Jio World Centre** (Mukesh Ambani / Reliance Industries) are excluded. Every Reliance-linked name below is a **different Reliance entity or a different Ambani** (Anil Ambani's ADAG is unrelated to Mukesh Ambani's RIL) — none is JioStar or Jio World Centre — but flagged anyway since the brand carries the same risk.
- Two names below (**Filmfare Middle East**, **TEDxGateway**) have real limits on how well they fit WAiFF's revenue-share model — flagged in their rows, not hidden.

## Top 10 by fit

1. **Zee Entertainment Enterprises Ltd**: the only name here that has literally run an **awards show** — WAiFF's own format — across seven international cities over nearly a decade, plus a live, currently-running international sponsored show (Sa Re Ga Ma Pa UK, 11 brands, Sep 2026). Biggest revenue base on the list too. Confirm the ZEE5-exclusion boundary before contacting.
2. **Knight Riders Sports / Red Chillies (Shah Rukh Khan)**: the best strategic fit for a *film* festival — Bollywood's biggest star, majority owner of a sports group that genuinely operates on four continents, built its own stadium in LA. Mumbai HQ, one direct email.
3. **Bennett Coleman & Co Ltd / The Times Group**: already the **official Cannes Lions country representative for India** — the exact kind of relationship WAiFF's Cannes-integration tier is selling. Largest media conglomerate in the country.
4. **JSW Group / JSW Sports**: $23B conglomerate, Mumbai HQ, proven multi-sport IP operator with one real international team (SA20) and a named, reachable sponsorship inbox.
5. **Mahindra Group**: runs its own branded festival in Mumbai (Blues Festival, 14 editions) and abroad (Fusion Fest South Africa) under one cultural-outreach umbrella — structurally the closest match to "hosts events across the globe and also in India."
6. **Yash Raj Films**: the only candidate with staffed, named-contact offices on three continents (UK, US, UAE, Canada) — deep film-industry credibility for a film festival specifically.
7. **Condé Nast India**: a working, monetised example of an international award franchise (GQ/Vogue) being run as a local Mumbai event — precisely WAiFF's own model, one level removed.
8. **Fashion Design Council of India**: two flagship national IP franchises and 400+ member brands, but weaker sourcing on the "international" and "contact" columns — verify before prioritising.
9. **Sun Group / Sun TV Network**: the cleanest India+international sister-brand structure (Sunrisers Hyderabad / Sunrisers Eastern Cape) on this list, undercut only by having no confirmed Mumbai presence.
10. **TEDxGateway**: real scale and a real international license, but flag the TED non-profit/anti-commercialisation licensing terms honestly before spending outreach time here — it may not be able to take WAiFF's deal structure at all.

**Positioning note:** Nazara Technologies (#11) is included for completeness but is one ownership level removed from actually running events (that's NODWIN, already contacted in the 19 Sep round) — deprioritise unless you specifically want to go up the chain to Nazara's corporate-development team instead of NODWIN's.

**Honesty note on count:** this list has 11 candidates, short of the 15–20 aimed for. The "big enough for Mumbai AND genuinely runs/licenses IP internationally" combination is a narrow slice of the Indian market — most of India's biggest experiential/event agencies (Wizcraft, Exhicon-adjacent names) are excluded, most WPP/Omnicom/dentsu India units are ad agencies without ticketed-event IP of their own (their event work mostly shows up through client-owned brands like Mahindra's or through agencies like Fountainhead, already on the 19 Sep list), and several plausible names (Filmfare Middle East's licensing status, FDCI's specific international partners, T-Series' claimed live-event footprint, India Today Conclave's claimed UK edition) did not hold up to source-checking and were dropped rather than padded in.
