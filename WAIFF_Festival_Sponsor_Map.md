# WAiFF India: map of who sponsors AI film, creative and gaming events

Compiled 19 Sep 2026. Sources are WebSearch results plus WebFetch reads of URLs that appeared in those results. No linkedin.com pages were used.
Rule followed: a sponsor is listed only if a fetched or searched page named it, and the URL is given for each one. If an event's sponsor list could not be found, the entry says so.
Exclusion check: every candidate name was compared as a substring against `/tmp/waiff/exclusion_str.txt` (545 entries), then each match was reviewed by hand.

## Insights

1. **AI-tool and model companies are by far the most frequent sponsors of AI film events**, but almost all of the big ones are already on the exclusion list. The remaining AI-tool pool is long-tail (Chroma/Odyssey Gold-to-Bronze tiers) and pays mostly in **credits, not cash**.
2. **Hardware makers show up at gaming and esports events, not AI film festivals.** Across more than 20 AI film events checked, only NVIDIA (Runway AIF) and HP (Dubai AI Festival) sponsored. That suggests a first-mover opening for hardware brands, but most are already excluded.
3. **Cash sponsors of India youth and creator culture are phones, energy drinks and autos**: OnePlus, realme, iQOO, HONOR; Red Bull, Monster, Campa; Maruti Suzuki, Yamaha, TVS. None of them sponsors an AI film event yet. They are the best new-money segment for WAiFF Mumbai, pitched as "Gen-Z creators plus AI plus Cannes" rather than "an AI festival".
4. **India's only corporate AI-cinema sponsor at scale is LTIMindtree** (IFFI AI Film Festival and AI Impact Summit showcase). It is the single highest-likelihood target.
5. **The Gulf and China fund AI film heavily.** Google Gemini ($1M at 1BF), Dubai government bodies, Kling (Beijing), Dreamina (Shanghai), MiniMax (WAiFF and Inspiring Asia), and Saudi brands (Qiddiya, NEOM, AlUla) are courting Indian audiences through Comic Con and MAMI.
6. **Film festivals with a tech angle mostly draw lifestyle, airline and fintech sponsors** (Tribeca: OKX, Citi, KLM, Chanel; MAMI: Turkish Airlines, Royal Stag, M·A·C). Airline and hospitality slots (Emirates, Turkish, Hilton) are realistic in-kind deals for a Mumbai-to-Cannes story.
7. **Gaps with no sponsor list found:** WAVES 2025 main sponsors, IndiaJoy full wall, IGDC, IGX brands, VidCon, TwitchCon San Diego, gamescom asia, Kling NextGen, AI Film Fest Amsterdam, AI Film Awards (KM Universe), India AI Film Awards / BHAI.
