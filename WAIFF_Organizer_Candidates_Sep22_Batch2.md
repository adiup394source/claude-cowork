**Overall read:** IPL franchise ownership turned out to be the richest vein — a category of large Indian corporates that have already proven they'll write nine/ten-figure cheques to own a large public sporting/entertainment property, exactly the profile the brief describes. Weak categories, deprioritized: IT services beyond TCS/Infosys/HCL/Wipro/TechM (LTIMindtree, Persistent, Coforge, Mphasis, Cyient have money, no verifiable event-organizing track record); auto/FMCG "flagship event" plays outside what's listed (Maruti Suzuki, Kia India, Coca-Cola India, Toyota Kirloskar have money but are sponsors, not organizers, of anything sourceable); real estate/mall developers and most large private banks came back with no citable owned event property.
