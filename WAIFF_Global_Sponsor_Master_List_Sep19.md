## What this means practically

1. **CloudWalk is the one lead in this entire document you should chase first** — it already has a commercial relationship with WAiFF. Worth asking the global WAiFF team directly whether that sponsorship desk can be looped in for India.
2. **In-kind (product, not cash) sponsors are the fastest wins available right now**: DXRacer/AndaSeat (chairs), Gunnar Optiks (eyewear), G.SKILL/ADATA (memory), Synology/QNAP (storage), PNY (GPUs) all already run active sponsorship programs and are used to saying yes to exactly this kind of ask.
3. **The Chinese-AI-company opening is narrower than it looked** — the big names are already in play; SenseTime, Meitu and Kunlun Tech/Skywork AI are the real new targets, and all three have a live "we need international credibility right now" story that fits WAiFF's Cannes-plus-emerging-market pitch well.
4. **Telecom is a category the first pass missed almost entirely** — Santander, Zain, Movistar and Claro all fund regional esports leagues; none has India relevance directly, but it's a useful precedent if you ever want to approach an Indian telecom not already on your list.
