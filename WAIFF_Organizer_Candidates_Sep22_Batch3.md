**Caveat on #9/#10:** multiple Sep 2025-2026 reports (Outlook India, ESPN, Al Jazeera) describe the Indian Super League itself as currently in serious institutional turmoil with AIFF/FSDL — treat the football-club-ownership hook as weakened for now; if pursued, lead with the parent company's own venue/event business instead.

**Overall read:** the strongest, most defensible tier is the three global exhibition operators (Informa Markets India, RX India, Messe Frankfurt India) plus the two tyre majors (MRF, JK Tyre) — all have hard, sourced revenue and a genuinely verifiable, years-to-decades-long track record of organizing large events in India on their own account, exactly the "serious company, has money, has done this before" test. RX India has the single best contact channel found in this pass (a named person with direct email). No contact channel was independently confirmed for 6 of the 10 — that verification is still needed before drafting anything for those.
