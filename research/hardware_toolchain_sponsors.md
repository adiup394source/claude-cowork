# WAiFF India: the AI-filmmaker workstation toolchain as a sponsor map

Compiled 19 Sep 2026. Logic: an AI filmmaker sits at a workstation; every link in that
hardware/software chain is a company whose target buyer is exactly WAiFF's audience of
AI-film creators. This file works through 12 toolchain categories and names companies not
already in `/tmp/waiff/exclusion_str.txt` (~545 entries checked by hand, substring + parent-brand
review). It complements, not replaces, `festival_sponsor_map.md` (the event-sponsor pass).

Note on the exclusion list: it already carries almost every top-tier PC-hardware name
(ASUS, MSI, Gigabyte, Lenovo, Acer, HP, Dell, Corsair, Razer, Samsung, Sony, NVIDIA, AMD,
Intel, Logitech, BenQ, ViewSonic, EIZO, Kingston, Micron, SK Hynix, Seagate, Western
Digital/SanDisk, Sennheiser, Harman/JBL, RODE, boAt, Noise, SteelSeries, Wacom,
Blackmagic Design, Canon, Adobe, and the major cloud/AI names). That is why most
category leaders are missing below — they were already found and logged in the first
pass. What follows is the next tier down: real, cash-spending companies whose target
customer is a video editor, VFX artist or AI-film creator, and who are not yet on the list.

## Insights

1. **The exclusion list has already absorbed almost the entire hardware "A-list"** — every
   major laptop, GPU, motherboard, RAM, monitor and audio brand with global scale is on it.
   Everything above is the credible next tier: real companies with confirmed sponsorship or
   creator-marketing budgets, just one rung down from the household names.
2. **The single closest precedent found in this whole pass is PNY Technologies sponsoring
   the "Best Short" category at the animago VFX/animation awards** — a hardware company
   directly funding a short-film category, which is the exact WAiFF ask.
3. **Clip Studio Paint (CELSYS) shows the clearest, most repeatable pattern**: it sponsors
   anime/comic festivals on nearly every continent (Brazil, Spain, Canada, UK) in 2026 alone.
   It is the strongest single lead for an AI-anime-film sponsorship pitch.
4. **Esports/gaming-chair and gaming-monitor money is real and already flowing to
   ergonomics/screen brands that have never touched film** (Herman Miller, AndaSeat,
   noblechairs, AOC, Gunnar Optiks) — these brands are used to buying "creator lifestyle"
   association and could plausibly extend that into an AI-film-festival lounge or
   jury-hospitality sponsorship.
5. **GPU-cloud is the thinnest category** once the AI-native names are excluded — DigitalOcean/
   Paperspace, OVHcloud and Vultr are legitimate but are infrastructure-first companies with
   less obvious cultural-marketing instinct than the AI-tool sponsors in the first-pass file.
