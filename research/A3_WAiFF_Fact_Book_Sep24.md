## A. Official name check

**Finding: the festival's name is "World AI Film Festival". The abbreviation is used both as "WAIFF" and "WAiFF" in official material. "World AI International Film Festival" is not used by WAiFF anywhere.** The brief's formulation "World AI Film Festival (WAiFF)" is correct and should stay.

Evidence (quoted):

|---|---|

| Licensing Agreement, Art. 3.4 protected identifiers (L10 p3) | "WAIFF; World AI Film Festival; World A.I. Film Festival; Institut EuropIA; or any confusingly similar designation." (the name list WAiFF itself protects) |
