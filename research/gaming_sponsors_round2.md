# WAiFF Round 2: Global gaming-award, esports and game-dev sponsors

Compiled 19 Sep 2026, as a follow-up to `/tmp/waiff/research/festival_sponsor_map.md` (96 targets, India/Asia-weighted Part 2). This pass went global — North America, Europe, Latin America, Middle East and China esports scenes — looking at major game awards (The Game Awards, BAFTA Games Awards, Golden Joystick, D.I.C.E., Esports Awards), major leagues/tournaments (LoL Worlds, LEC/LCS/LCK/LLA/CBLOL, Valorant, CS2 (BLAST/ESL/IEM), Rocket League, Apex, Overwatch, PUBG, Free Fire, Mobile Legends, Rainbow Six, Fortnite), and conferences (GDC, gamescom, PAX, DreamHack), with a focus on laptop/PC/GPU/monitor brands, gaming chairs, headsets, energy drinks, telecom/5G, streaming platforms and AI/software sponsors.

**Method:** WebSearch plus WebFetch of official event/sponsor pages and esports trade press (Esports Insider, The Esports Advocate, esports.gg, Liquipedia, Wikipedia, official team/league sites). No LinkedIn pages used. Every candidate name was checked as a substring against `/tmp/waiff/exclusion_str.txt` (545 entries) and against the 96 names already in `festival_sponsor_map.md` Part 3 — none of the companies below appear in either. Where an event's full sponsor page could not be loaded (robots-blocked), that is noted and the company is sourced from press coverage instead.

- **JD.com (Jingdong), Cotti Coffee, RIO, Momchilovtsi, ZIWI** — named LoL Worlds China partners, but China-domestic brands with no discernible India footprint; listed in the events table above for completeness but not carried into the target list to avoid padding it with unsourced-fit guesses.
- **Volvo Construction Equipment** (DreamHack Stockholm 2025 partner) and **CHEXX** (ESL Pro League betting-platform partner) — real sponsors but a poor category fit for an AI film festival (heavy machinery; gambling), so excluded from the target list.
- **T-Mobile** was a long-time Overwatch League/CDL sponsor but ended that sponsorship (confirmed via multiple 2023-24 articles) — not included as a live lead.

## Insights

1. **Telecom is the clearest new-money category from this global pass.** Santander (banking), Zain, Movistar and Claro all sponsor League of Legends' regional leagues outside Asia — a pattern the India-focused first pass largely missed because it looked at BGMI/KRAFTON rather than Riot's global league structure.
2. **Gaming-chair and headset brands (DXRacer, AndaSeat, Turtle Beach, AutoFull) are realistic in-kind sponsors** — they already pay for physical product placement at esports LANs, which maps directly onto a jury lounge or green-room ask, unlike cash-only categories.
3. **Insurance companies (State Farm, Progressive) and Michelin sponsoring US/EU esports leagues show gaming-adjacent sponsorship now extends well past hardware and drinks** — useful precedent when pitching Indian insurers or auto-ancillary brands not yet in either list.
4. **Nintendo and Kick stand out as bigger-name gets**: Nintendo has no AI-film-festival association anywhere in the prior 96-company map, and Kick is a fast-growing streaming rival to Twitch that isn't excluded.
5. **Several major events yielded nothing new** — gamescom's and ALGS' official sponsor pages block automated fetches (robots.txt), Golden Joystick's page had only logo images with no legible sponsor names in the fetched text, and PAX, GDC, D.I.C.E. Awards and IEM Katowice/Kraków searches surfaced mostly team-level or previously-excluded sponsors. These are gaps worth a manual/browser-based follow-up if more names are needed.
