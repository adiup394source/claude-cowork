Grounded strictly in WAIFF_OPERATING_BRIEF.md section 2. No Canva/deck link ever, per section 3. Fill every [bracket] with two real, current facts about that specific company (a present initiative + an upcoming project) before sending — a shared "why" paragraph across companies is exactly what the user flagged as not good enough.

```

```

## Rules that apply to all three (never relax)
- No em dashes, no semicolons, no "moreover/furthermore/in today's landscape"
- Never promise a tier, price, benefit, audience figure, or Marco Landi's personal attendance (organizer-track €50k/€400k figures are the sole named-price exception, per brief section 3)
- Never send the Canva deck link or presentation file, in any channel, ever
- Say "December 2026," never the exact date, externally
- Always mention IST when proposing a call
- The opening specific-fact line is the whole message - if you can't find two real facts (present + upcoming) about the company, it isn't researched enough to send yet
