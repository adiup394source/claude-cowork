## 2. Why we are failing: 8 causes, most damaging first

**1. We were selling an event nobody can buy yet.** The India edition has no public date, venue, organizer or audience number. A brand manager cannot put "India edition, December, details TBA" through approval. Meanwhile we had a fully dated product: **Cannes, 6-7 April 2027, Palais des Festivals, third edition**, plus Buenos Aires, Montreal/Vancouver, LA, London, Seoul, Japan and China. Global sponsors should buy Cannes plus editions. India is the add-on for India-focused brands.

**2. We reached the wrong desk.** A large share of sends went to press@, support@, info@, investor relations, legal@, the company secretary, customer care, a campus-recruiting inbox (ASML), a distributor (Nanlite via Foto Centre) or all-BCC blasts (Dell's board inbox, HP, Logitech, ViewSonic). These desks cannot approve sponsorship, so they route it to a bot or say no. Anthropic's PR agency and NVIDIA's PR both said exactly this. The replies that went somewhere came from named marketing, partnership or sales people.

**3. We targeted companies without a sponsorship habit.** Most sends went to AI startups and hardware makers. The startups have no budget. Their own words: "as a startup we're careful with capacity" (Wonder), "our budget for financial event sponsorships is limited" (Cascadeur), "no dedicated marketing resource" (Ideogram), "focusing on our inference business" (Hedra). Hardware giants run 6-9 month global procurement. Companies that **already pay** film festivals, Bollywood awards and Cannes were barely touched: L'Oréal, Air France, Magnum, the jewellers, Gulf real-estate developers, airlines and tourism boards.

**4. There were visible errors in the emails.** The 13-14 Sep template said *"Cascadeur's India edition is launching in Mumbai"* and *"Ideogram AI's India edition..."*, a merge bug that made the festival sound like theirs. Other problems:
- "Apple Global" and "the same venue as the Cannes Film Festival" appeared (the brief bans the second).
- "This is moving fast, I'd rather bring this to you before it's fully allocated" is false urgency.
- The 21 Sep Hedra reply called it the "World AI **International** Film Festival" and listed Moscow/Berlin editions that aren't on the official map.
- Hedra's CEO replied that the follow-up was so AI-written he found it hard to read.
- Kimi got a 1,500-word rebuttal to a polite no.

Senior people notice all of this.

**5. We pitched organizers who have no reason to organize.** We asked the company secretaries of a tyre maker (MRF), a steel company, a pharma group and a cement/cricket group to pay €50,000 to run a film festival. MRF said "doesn't fit into our plans." Organizers must be companies whose **business is running events and selling sponsorship**: festival owners, event IP owners, AI-event media and government-backed festival hosts.

**6. Warm leads were left without a reply.** This is the most fixable cause, and it cost the most:
- **NESCO (Bombay Exhibition Centre)** asked for a call on 22 Sep. No reply.
- **Cascadeur** asked for options on 14 Sep and offered product cooperation. No reply.
- **Getty Images** wrote on 11 Sep (starred). No reply.
- **Novotel Juhu** asked for requirements on 18 Sep. No reply.
- **Trident BKC** said on 11 Sep that your phone was unreachable. No reply.
- **Yashobhoomi** asked three times before being dropped.

**7. Our credibility signals are weak.** We send from a personal @gmail.com with no official WAiFF India page or email. Many B2B forms reject Gmail outright (Sarvam, Ideogram, Colossyan...). Compliance teams at big brands hesitate too. Ask Marco's team for an **@worldaifilmfestival.com mailbox** and a short **appointment letter (PDF)**. Nothing else on this list is as cheap for the result it gets.

**8. We contacted existing WAiFF partners.** TF1, Fnac, Station F, ByteDance/CapCut and MiniMax were all emailed, and CloudWalk already sponsors WAiFF Brazil. No commission is payable on these, and it can look bad to HQ. Stop there.

## 3. What changes from today

1. **Reply first, then send new mail.** Start with the "Warm leads - reply now" sheet. Five doors are open.
2. **Sell the dated product.** Pitch Cannes 2027 (6-7 Apr) plus named editions to global brands. Offer India (December) as "founding partner of the first India edition".
3. **One concrete offer per company.** For example: official airline, official beauty partner, a named award, destination partner, or official photo agency.
4. **Put a named person in TO** (from your Apollo pull). Press inboxes go in BCC only.
5. **Short, human replies.** 5-8 lines. No essays, no AI tone.
6. **Use the India-France Year of Innovation 2026.** Macron and Modi opened it at the Gateway of India, and "Cultural & Creative Industries" is one of its four pillars. WAiFF is French-born, so this is the strongest door we have. It opens Capgemini (platinum partner), BNP Paribas, L'Oréal, Air France, Dassault, Publicis and the Institut Français.
7. **Use the competitor rule.** CapCut has an award at WAiFF Cannes, so pitch Picsart. JW Marriott sponsors Cannes, so pitch Hilton and Jumeirah. Lotte is a WAiFF partner, so pitch CJ ENM.
