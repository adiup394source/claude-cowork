## 2. Why nothing has closed in 15 days (the honest diagnosis)

1. **Chicken and egg.** Sponsors ask for names, a date, a venue and an organizer. Organizers ask for sponsors. Right now India has none of the four. No amount of extra email volume fixes this. Only putting one anchor in place does.
2. **Most cold email lands with the wrong person.** Most of the 600+ emails went to press, support, sales or investor inboxes. Those people can only forward. A forward with no named champion dies in a queue, which is exactly what the auto-acks and "forwarded to the relevant team" replies show.
3. **The ask is fuzzy.** One email offers sponsor, organizer and global partner at once. A busy reader can't say yes to three things. Each email should make one ask that fits that company.
4. **Warm replies are going cold.** Cascadeur asked a direct question and has waited 5 days. Warm replies are the only real currency here, and they need a same-day answer.
5. **Volume is working against deliverability.** At 70 emails a day with 10 to 20 BCC each, one account is sending to over 1,000 recipients a day. Personal Gmail limits are about 500 recipients per 24 hours. Mass BCC and bounces from guessed or old addresses push the account toward spam folders, so every future email lands worse.

**Realistic 5-day target.** A signed organizer in 5 days is unlikely from cold email alone. What is achievable is 4 to 6 qualified calls and 2 or 3 in-kind partners you can name publicly. That gives you one organizer or presenting-partner conversation at the proposal stage, and those three things are what unlock everything else.
