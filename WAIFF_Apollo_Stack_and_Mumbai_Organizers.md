### A3. Ranked stack: the best 5 finders for maximum free verified emails per week (personal-Gmail team)

The ranking assumes the team signs up with personal Gmail addresses. That rules out Lusha and MillionVerifier's free credits, and Apollo's larger corporate-domain allowance. Rank is by weekly yield multiplied by verification quality and ease of use.

| Rank | Tool | Approx. verified emails per week | Why it is in the stack |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Apollo.io** | about 18–25 reveals (about 75–100/mo) | Best **search and filter** engine for finding names and titles; also feeds the other tools |
| 2 | **ContactOut** | about 25–35 (about 4–5/day, used daily) | Largest recurring free volume. Works on LinkedIn profiles, so the team uses it in their own browser |
| 3 | **Prospeo** | about 17 (75/mo, plus extension credits) | High claimed accuracy; verification costs only 0.5 credit |
| 4 | **GetProspect** | about 12 (50 valid/mo, rollover) + 100 verifications | Only charges for valid emails, **free CSV export**, and credits roll over |
| 5 | **Hunter.io** | about 12 (50 credits/mo) | Domain search shows a company's email pattern (e.g. first.last@), so you can guess other addresses and verify them in Reoon |
| Verification layer | **Reoon** (20/day, bulk) + **ZeroBounce** (100/mo) | about 140 + 25 verifications/week | Re-check every guessed or "accept-all" address before sending |
| Bench | Snov.io (50/mo, no export), Kaspr (15+5/mo), Tomba (25/mo), Wiza (20/mo), Clay (100 data credits/mo) | +about 25/wk combined | Add these once the top 5 are exhausted |

**Total: about 85–100 found emails a week, plus about 165 verifications a week, at zero cost.**

**Workflow:**
1. Find names in Apollo.
2. Reveal emails in ContactOut, Prospeo, GetProspect or Hunter.
3. Where a reveal fails, construct the address from Hunter's email pattern.
4. Verify every address in Reoon.
5. Send only to "valid". Treat "catch-all" as risky.

**ToS warning:** Hunter says explicitly that you may only have one account. Snov.io prohibits multiple Trial accounts, including separate accounts for several people at the same company. **Do not create multiple accounts per tool.** A small team gets one legitimate account per person only where the vendor allows it, and Snov.io does not allow it.

### A4. Sending from a personal Gmail: safe numbers

| Limit | Figure | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Daily sending cap (free Gmail) | **500 messages / about 500 recipients per rolling 24 h**; going over locks sending for up to 24 h (error `550 5.4.5 Daily user sending quota exceeded`) | [mailjerry](https://www.mailjerry.com/gmail-limits/), [folk.app](https://www.folk.app/articles/gmail-limit-101-common-error-messages-and-daily-send-limits), [inboxally](https://www.inboxally.com/blog/how-many-recipients-does-gmail-allow) |
| Recipients per message | Web UI: up to 500 (To + Cc + Bcc). About **100 per message** is widely reported for SMTP/mail clients and some accounts | same sources (they conflict, so assume 100) |
| Bulk-sender threshold | About **5,000 messages/day** to Gmail users puts you in the "bulk sender" regime | [Google sender guidelines FAQ](https://support.google.com/mail/answer/14229414?hl=en) |
| Spam-complaint rate | Stay **below 0.1%**; never reach **0.3%** | same Google FAQ |
| Workspace comparison | 2,000/day | [Google Workspace limits](https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/gmail/gmail-sending-limits-in-google-workspace) |

I could not fetch Google's own personal-Gmail limits page (support.google.com/mail/answer/22839) because it was blocked. The 500/day figure comes from several third-party sources that agree with each other.

**Recommended operating limits for cold B2B outreach from a personal Gmail.** These are best practice, not Google rules:
- **Weeks 1–2:** 20–30 new cold emails a day. **Weeks 3+:** up to 40–50 a day. Keep total sends, including replies, well under 200 a day.
- **Send one-to-one.** Put one recipient in "To". **Never BCC a batch of prospects.** A large BCC looks like bulk mail, lowers inbox placement, triggers spam complaints and gives each prospect a non-personal email.
- Space sends 1–3 minutes apart and personalise the first line.
- Verify every address first and keep bounces under 2%. Bounces and spam reports are what get personal accounts limited.
- Include a plain opt-out line. Stop mailing anyone who asks, within 48 h (Google asks bulk senders to honour unsubscribes within 48 h).
