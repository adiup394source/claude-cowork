# WAiFF Mail Quality

**25 Sep 2026 override:** the email format now follows `WAIFF_Email_Writer_Prompt_FINAL.md` and brief section 22:
- Greeting "Hi there team,".
- The fixed WAiFF blocks, which make emails longer.
- TO plus BCC, with the company's own PR, media or partnerships inbox allowed in BCC.
- No phone number.

Where this skill says otherwise on those four points, section 22 wins. Everything else here (verified facts, banned claims, no em dashes, no prices or decks, research first, reply standard) still applies.

Read `WAIFF_Email_Quality_Playbook_Sep24.md` (repo root) before writing. It is the standard, built from an audit of ~990 sent emails, a sourced best-practice study, and a verified WAiFF fact book. `WAIFF_OPERATING_BRIEF.md` section 2 (corrections block) and section 21 point to it.

## Before writing
1. The company is on `WAIFF_Target_Companies_v5_Sep24.xlsx`, or has passed the same test: tech-led, money for $100k-$450k, proof it already sponsors big events, not in any tracker, not do-not-contact. Organizers must be big, with event muscle and a reason to want a Cannes-linked IP now.
2. The recipient is a named decision-maker, never press@, media@, support@, care@, info@, helpdesk@, investor relations or the company secretary. The address is verified and has never bounced.
3. The company has never had a WAiFF email under another subject. One company, one sequence. No CC, no BCC.

## Writing (first email)
- Subject: 2 to 5 words, lowercase, names them or one asset. No "opportunity", "partnership", "sponsorship", "X x WAiFF", em dash or fake "Re:".
- Body, 50 to 90 words (organizers up to 110): (1) a dated fact about them, (2) one WAiFF fact, (3) one idea built for them, (4) one proof point, (5) an interest question ("Worth a look?").
- Only facts from playbook Part C1. Never anything in C2 (no "100+ countries", "15+ countries/30+ cities", "third Cannes edition", "Jury President Gong Li", Hollywood names, Air France as sponsor, "Cannes Film Festival", "India edition confirmed", "Mumbai", "15 December", price, deck, ROI).
- No em dashes, semicolons, exclamation marks or AI phrases ("I hope this email finds you well", "truly remarkable", "journey", "Hi team" to a named person).
- Signature: Aditya Upadhyay / India Ambassador, World AI Film Festival / worldaifilmfestival.com (plain text, never a google.com/url redirect) / +91 mobile (WhatsApp). One opt-out line.

## Follow-ups
Same thread, 30 to 60 words, each adds something new. Sponsor: day 3 second idea, day 8 real date (entries close 15 Feb 2027), day 17 breakup and referral. Organizer: day 3 revenue formula, day 6 deadline (60-day validation), day 10 breakup. Never "following up on the note below". Never after a decline or a bounce.

## Replies
Answer every human reply within 24 hours, before any new outreach. Answer the exact question in 120 words or less, written by hand. Send the calendar invite yourself for a specific slot in their time zone. Never a one-liner, never an essay, never argue with a decline.

## Sending
20 to 40 new emails a day, weekdays, 8 to 11am in their time zone, schedule-send, no bursts. Log every send the same day.

## Output check
Run the pre-send checklist (playbook Part J) on every draft and fix any failure before showing it to Aditya. Never send without Aditya's go-ahead for that batch.
