## 1. What WAiFF is, and where it came from

The World AI Film Festival was founded in 2025 by Marco Landi, the former President and Chief Operating Officer of Apple worldwide, who was part of the task force that brought Steve Jobs back to Apple in 1997. It was established through the Institut EuropIA together with the Departement des Alpes-Maritimes.

The inaugural edition ran in Nice in April 2025 with over 1,500 submissions. From 2026 the flagship moved to the Palais des Festivals in Cannes, the same building that hosts the Cannes Film Festival. To be precise, because people reasonably assume otherwise, WAiFF is an independent festival and is not the Cannes Film Festival. It is its own event, in that venue.

The 2026 Cannes edition ran on 21 and 22 April across four venues. The Palais des Festivals for the galas and awards, Espace Miramar for the Pro Talks, Cinema Les Arcades for public screenings, and the JW Marriott for networking. It drew more than 6,000 applications from over 100 countries, brought together more than 5,000 participants, put 80 projects into official competition and granted 13 awards.

The 2027 flagship is fixed for 6 and 7 April 2027 at the Palais des Festivals.

Leadership for 2026 was Gong Li as Festival President, Claude Lelouch as Honorary President, Agnes Jaoui as Jury President and Jean-Michel Jarre as Ambassador. The 2026 jury included Aissa Maiga, Roger Avary, Elsa Zylberstein, Ruby Yang, Joanna Popper, Serge Hayat, Nam Na-young, Reza Sixo Safai and Kavan Cardoza. Mathieu Kassovitz spoke at the Pro Talks. Ron Howard, James Cameron and Matthew McConaughey have engaged with the festival. I will say engaged rather than endorsed, because I would rather be precise with you than impressive.

The institutional backing is real and it is public. The Departement des Alpes-Maritimes, whose President Charles Ange Ginesy is with us, and the City of Cannes under Mayor David Lisnard. When I say the Mayor of Cannes is with us, that is a matter of public record on the festival's own team page, not a figure of speech.

Press coverage has included Screen Daily, Le Monde, Le Figaro, Paris Match and BFM Business, plus a live special on CCTV-6 in China.

## 2. Why this is not a niche event, and why the timing is what it is

I want to give you the industry numbers rather than adjectives, because you are going to have to take this to people who will ask.

The AI filmmaking market was valued at about 3.2 billion dollars in 2024 and is forecast to reach roughly 23.5 billion by 2033, a compound growth rate of about 25 percent a year, according to Grand View Research. AI across media and entertainment more broadly is forecast to go from about 33.7 billion dollars in 2025 to around 159 billion by 2033. I am citing those as one research firm's estimates rather than as settled fact, but the direction is not in dispute.

What is not an estimate is the volume. In January 2026 China was releasing roughly 470 AI-generated short dramas every single day. A new AI-generated micro-drama was going live on a Chinese streaming platform roughly every 90 seconds. Around 50,000 AI-native titles were added to Douyin in March 2026 alone. Most tellingly, AI titles went from 7 percent of China's top 100 micro-drama chart in January 2025 to 38 percent in January 2026. That is a twelve-month move, and it is audiences choosing, not platforms promoting.

The economics driving that are brutal and simple. AI-assisted short-drama production cuts cost by 80 to 90 percent and compresses the timeline from three or four months to under one month, with over 90 percent of AI-generated footage usable. FlexTV has stopped traditionally shot production entirely. Netflix confirmed publicly that a building-collapse sequence in The Eternaut was completed with generative AI roughly ten times faster than conventional visual effects, and could not have been afforded otherwise.

The festival layer has grown to match. There are now more than 90 active AI film festivals and awards worldwide, almost all launched from 2024 onward. Runway's AI Film Festival went from around 300 submissions in 2023 to more than 6,000 in 2025, roughly twentyfold in two years. Ours took over 6,000 applications from more than 100 countries in a single edition.

This is the part I would ask you to sit with. An entire production discipline is being built right now, in public, by people who did not come up through the studio system. They are choosing their tools this year and next. Nobody has told them what the standard is, because for this generation nobody has been in the room to tell them.

## 3. The question you actually asked

You said, fairly, that Sennheiser already has audio mixing and sound designers, you ship to production teams, and the work gets done. So what does a festival add.

Here is my honest answer, and it is the reason I wanted this meeting more than most.

Sound is the unsolved problem in AI filmmaking, and everybody in the field knows it.

In December 2025, Index Ventures and Andreessen Horowitz led a 41 million dollar seed round into a company called Mirelo, whose entire founding thesis is that AI video has no sound. Forty-one million dollars, at seed, for the silence problem. Their chief executive put it this way, and I think it is the single best line anyone has said about this industry. "Sound is 50 percent of the movie-going experience. It's not an overstatement. If anything, it's an understatement."

In February 2026, ElevenLabs raised 500 million dollars at an 11 billion dollar valuation on more than 330 million dollars of annual recurring revenue. The AI voice and audio generation market is compounding at roughly 29.5 percent a year, which is faster than AI video generation at 20.3 percent. Audio is growing faster than the picture it serves.

And the industry itself predicted this. In the CVL Economics study of 300 entertainment executives, only 47 percent believed generative AI would effectively handle sound design by 2026. Music generation and mastering scored lowest of everything measured, at 39 percent. Executives were confident AI would take the image. They were not confident it would take the sound.

Which leaves a very specific gap. There is now an enormous, fast-growing population of filmmakers who can generate a convincing image in an afternoon and cannot make it sound like anything. They will solve that with microphones, with capture, with monitoring, with immersive mixing, and with people who understand acoustics. That is your entire business.

The hardware signal confirms it. Futuresource surveyed more than 16,000 people across eight countries including India. There were 246 million online video creators in 2025, heading to 267 million by 2030. Nearly half of creators who own accessories have spent over 1,000 dollars on equipment and 70 percent have spent over 500. The single most commonly planned purchase across that entire population is microphones. Ahead of lenses, ahead of gimbals, ahead of cameras.

So the festival does not help you make audio. You are better at that than anyone. The festival puts Sennheiser in the room at the exact moment a new generation of filmmakers works out that the picture is free and the sound is the hard part.

## 4. Why CapCut is a global partner, and why JW Marriott is

You asked, indirectly, why a company like yours belongs here. Let me answer it with the two partners we already have, because they answer it better than I can.

CapCut is an editing tool. It is not a film studio, it is not a distributor, it is not a production house. It is a piece of software in the workflow. It is one of our global sponsors, and there is a CapCut Prize awarded at Cannes, because in filmmaking the tools are not a supporting cast. They are the craft. Whoever is in the hand of the creator at the moment of making is the brand that generation grows up loyal to.

JW Marriott is the one I usually use, because it makes the point cleanly. A hotel sponsors a film festival. Why. Because the association is with the high end of the craft, with an international audience, with a level of service and prestige that no advertisement buys. I am sitting in India and I know that JW Marriott is in Cannes. That is what the association delivers, and it delivers it across every market the festival touches, not just the one the hotel sits in.

Sennheiser is a stronger fit than either, and I mean that literally. CapCut cuts the picture. JW Marriott hosts the people. Sennheiser makes the thing that is actually missing from the work being submitted.

## 5. Why I want it to be Sennheiser specifically

You asked me to make the case, given we have options. So let me make it, and let me make it in your own language rather than mine.

Sennheiser received the Academy Scientific and Engineering Award in 1987 for the MKH 816 shotgun microphone. The Television Academy gave Sennheiser the Philo T. Farnsworth Engineering Emmy in 2013, an award given irregularly to organisations whose contribution has significantly shaped how television is made. Neumann, which has been part of the Sennheiser Group since 1991, took a Technical GRAMMY in 1999 for creating the world's first mass-produced condenser microphone, and the U 47 fet entered the NAMM TECnology Hall of Fame this January.

The MKH 416 launched in 1975 and turned fifty last year. It has had two revisions in fifty years. It is still the default for location sound, for ADR and for voiceover, and the story of Dr Hans-Joachim Griese taking a hacksaw to the interference tube of a 415 prototype because broadcasters found it too directional is exactly the kind of origin story this audience responds to. AI filmmakers are engineers who became storytellers. They love that story.

And you are already in this fight on the distribution side. AMBEO 2-Channel Spatial Audio powers Netflix's Premium tier across more than 700 titles including Stranger Things, Wednesday and Glass Onion. What matters to a festival audience is not that it works, it is that it respects creative intent, because mixers control the amount of processing per stem and can leave dialogue untouched while spatialising ambience. That is a philosophical position about authorship, and it happens to be the exact anxiety that hangs over AI filmmaking.

Your own chief executive said something in March that I have quoted more than once since. "We really want to focus on the professional side of the business and make sure that, whenever somebody gets serious about what they do, they have Sennheiser on the radar." There are several hundred thousand people getting serious about AI filmmaking right now. Very few of them have Sennheiser on the radar yet, because nobody has put it there.

And when you announced Formula E last month, Dr Sennheiser said, "Great visuals deserve equally compelling sound, and we see real potential to define the sport's unique acoustic character." Everything in that sentence applies here with more force. AI filmmaking is a medium whose visuals arrived before its sound did. Its acoustic character is genuinely undefined. Somebody is going to define it in the next twenty-four months.

One more thing I noticed, and I mention it because it is the actual opportunity. Sennheiser's partnership portfolio is weighted to music, live events and now motorsport. Reeperbahn, Amsterdam Dance Event, fabric London, MusiCares, UNESCO Cities of Music, Formula E. Film festivals and film-sound awards are close to unoccupied for Sennheiser, and the Cinema Audio Society's top sponsorship sits with a competitor. You have a fifty-year claim on film sound and almost no cultural presence in film. I would like to fix that, and I would like to fix it in the fastest-growing corner of the medium rather than the slowest.

## 6. What Official Audio Partner actually unlocks

This is where I want to be concrete, because the documents I am sending are a starting point and not a ceiling.

**A named award.** This is established practice, not a hypothesis. There is already a CapCut Prize. Best AI Screenplay is presented with Genario and Best AI Micro-Series with Canneseries. A Sennheiser Award for Sound, or Best Sound Design, handed on stage at the Palais des Festivals and at every national edition, is a straightforward thing for us to create.

**A named competition strand.** An entire category of the festival, in every edition, carrying your name. Every submission to it is a filmmaker thinking about sound for the duration of the project.

**A named filmmaking hackathon.** We run hackathons for new and emerging AI filmmakers, and this is the format I would push hardest for you. A Sennheiser hackathon where teams are given a brief and have to deliver finished work, with sound as a judged criterion, puts your equipment in people's hands under deadline pressure. That is how tools get adopted. Not through advertising, through one good experience at three in the morning before a deadline.

**Masterclasses and workshops.** You already run the Sennheiser Sound Academy and you already build education into your partnerships. A sound-design masterclass strand at every edition, taught by your people or by people you choose, is the most natural extension of what you already do.

**Keynote at the Pro Talks.** Google keynoted at Cannes 2026 on copyright and AI. The slot exists and it is serious.

**Booths, demo spaces and product activations** at every edition, so people can put their hands on the gear rather than read about it.

**Access.** C-suite figures, festival talent, jury members, international organisers and press attend these events. The delegation at Cannes 2026 included around 130 representatives including judges, speakers and international organisers.

**Visibility across the whole network, not one event.** This is the part I most want you to weigh. Every national edition carries its own local and national press in its host market, on top of the international press around the Cannes flagship. A global partner is marketed across every one of those markets.

And the answer to "can we do something that is not on this list" is yes. We are flexible, and the honest position is that an organiser has the authority to structure and promise benefits for their own edition. So tell me what you want. If Sennheiser wants a sound-design residency, a student award, an acoustics lab at the venue, an immersive listening room running AMBEO, a Neumann monitoring suite for the finalists, a technical jury seat, or something I have not thought of, we build it. ## 7. The network, and why global rather than India alone

We run national editions through the year, each with its own local organising team, and several with their own websites. Korea runs at worldaifilmfestival.kr and the United States at worldaifilmfestival.us, with Istanbul, Abidjan and Berlin hosted on the main site.

The model is called Road to Cannes. Every national edition selects its best AI films, and those selections advance into the Road to Cannes Selection and compete at the Palais des Festivals finale. So a partner is not buying one evening. They are buying a year-long pipeline that finishes on the Cannes stage.

The published calendar into Cannes 2027 runs Berlin in September 2026, Buenos Aires on 30 October, Istanbul on 4 November, London on 24 November, Beijing in December, India in December 2026, Jordan in January 2027, Abidjan in February, Seoul on 18 and 19 March, Sao Paulo on 22 and 23 March, Tokyo in March, Los Angeles on 28 and 29 March, and Cannes on 6 and 7 April 2027.
